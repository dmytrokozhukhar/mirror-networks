Frequently Asked Questions

How does the audit process work?

Once your payment is approved, our security team begins the external diagnostic process within 24 hours. We conduct passive reconnaissance and vulnerability scanning strictly on the provided IP address. The final PDF report is delivered to your email within the specified SLA.

What exactly is included in the deliverable?

You will receive a highly concentrated, professionally formatted PDF document. It includes an executive summary, detailed technical findings (exposed ports, SSL/TLS cryptographic status), risk theory analysis, and specific, actionable firewall remediation steps.

We provide post-audit support. If your system administrators require clarification regarding the remediation steps or firewall configurations detailed in the report, email us directly before making structural network changes.
